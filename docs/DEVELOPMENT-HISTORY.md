# Burnice — Pixel Walk / Damage Study 04

Playable browser prototype: eight-way walking, four sprite facings, idle, directional sword slashes, hold-to-charge and release-to-spin attacks. Three regenerating training dummies show HP, floating damage and cumulative statistics. Thai interface, keyboard and simultaneous touch joystick / attack button, collision, depth sorting, fullscreen and animation inspector.

Static files are in `dist/`. No build step or runtime dependencies. The generated original is `dist/assets/burnice-generated.png`; 24 individual transparent 64×64 frames are in `dist/assets/frames/`. `burnice-atlas.json` records row order and anchor. The downloadable ZIP contains the original, atlas, metadata and individual frames.

Character frames were generated with the built-in Image Generation tool using a previously supplied Burnice character reference. `tools/slice_sprites.py` crops and aligns the output with nearest-neighbor reduction, without drawing replacement character art. Re-run with Pillow installed after replacing the source PNG. Environment tiles are drawn using canvas.

## Sword controls and damage

WASD or arrows move. J, Z or Space attacks; the sword button supports mouse and touch. Press starts a 0.34-second directional slash for 20 damage. Continue holding after the slash to charge for 0.8 seconds, at 42% movement speed. Charging turns toward movement and plays a generated walking cycle based on actual distance; stopping or pushing against scenery returns to a stationary charging pose. A meter signals readiness, and the blade aura continues pulsing even at full charge. Release a full charge for a 0.64-second spin dealing 60 damage in a circle. An early release cancels charging. Each attack damages each target only once. Blur, page hiding and pointer cancellation clear input; releasing the joystick does not cancel a separately held attack finger.

Dummies have 300 HP and physical collision. HP restores four seconds after the last hit, or after 2.2 seconds at zero HP. Reset controls restore targets and statistics. Slash and spin briefly lock movement.
